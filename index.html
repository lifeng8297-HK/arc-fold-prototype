Model Assumptions and Measurement Method

The comparison uses the fixed Ø900 mm round pedestal table from Table 10 of the project report. The folding table in the earlier reference photos uses a different construction.

  • Both tables are 900 mm in diameter and 740 mm high, with an 18 mm tabletop and a Ø70 mm pedestal. Each base arm reaches 380 mm from the centre and is about 40 mm wide. The model represents hardware with simplified solid shapes.
  • Revised 180° wall fit. This alternative adds an 85 mm sliding base carriage and two drop/slide rear hinges. The base shifts inward, the rear leaves lower 22 mm below the fixed top, slide 14 mm inward, and then fold. Default service gap is 8 mm. The 3 mm option is a geometry check. Fold the panels away from the wall before moving the table to its final position. The original report and BOM describe the previous construction; this revision adds hardware and has a separate HK$600 procurement target with a HK$610 ceiling; supplier quotes remain pending. The 18 mm tabletop, Ø70 mm post and full-size feet are retained. Two extra short rear pads stay on the floor when the rear arms rise. They and their crossbar remain inside the wall-side edge. New hardware, locks and stability require engineering and physical tests. The other three configurations retain their original mechanism. In 180° mode the lower Folding Base Arm close-up illustrates the original pivot only; use the main sequence to see the revised carriage and panel movement.
  • Original 180° wall fit. Fold the rear two panels and their arms, then move the table as close to the wall as the component shapes allow. Measure both tables at x = ±300 mm from the tabletop centreline. Concept A has a straight rear edge with a uniform gap. The round baseline has separate readings at the point of tangency and the two ends of the diameter.
  • 270° column fit. Lower one panel, raise the corresponding arm and move the column relative to the table along the opening’s 45° bisector. Final setback is the distance from the column’s outer edge to each radial edge of the opening. The model samples the path every 2 mm or less and calculates signed clearance between each component’s plan footprint and the full-height column. Negative values indicate approximate overlap in plan.
  • 90° corner fit. Lower the left and right tabletop panels first. Then fold the last panel to the right in one motion. It stacks beside the right panel with a 4 mm gap. One sector stays open above the arm along the corner bisector. This motion assumes sliding hinges and an extra pivot. Hardware and single-arm stability still need testing.
  • The folding animation uses radial hinges and simplified base-arm motion. The model flags overlap between the panels and the pedestal at its actual position. Locking, other internal clearances, load capacity and stability are outside the scope of this model.

Dimensions, travel paths and clearances are model estimates. Specification references include Table 10 and the project targets and Lesson 5.
