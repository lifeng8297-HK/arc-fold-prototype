概念简化与测量方法 / Concept simplifications and measurement

本页是概念摆放示意。180°靠墙与90°墙角视图压缩了折叠后部件的后侧轮廓，展示8 mm间隙；270°使用Ø240 mm圆柱与19.3 mm终点退让参数，计算柱边净空。没有新增底座滑轨或短后支撑脚。这些读数验证简化外形的摆放关系，完整机构的尺寸、干涉、承重及稳定性仍需详细设计。

The fixed Ø900 mm round table is the comparison baseline. The nominal tabletop thickness is 18 mm. In the simplified wall/corner views, rear silhouettes are compressed during the last part of folding. Those views do not preserve the original thickness and shape of every folded component. The 8 mm readings are concept placement checks.

  • Both tables are 900 mm in diameter and 740 mm high, with an 18 mm tabletop and a Ø70 mm pedestal. Each base arm reaches 380 mm from the centre and is about 40 mm wide. The model represents hardware with simplified solid shapes.
  • 180° wall fit. Fold the rear two panels and their arms, then move the table as close to the wall as the component shapes allow. Measure both tables at x = ±300 mm from the tabletop centreline. Concept A has a straight rear edge with a uniform gap. The round baseline has separate readings at the point of tangency and the two ends of the diameter.
  • 270° column fit. Lower one panel, raise the corresponding arm and move the column relative to the table along the opening’s 45° bisector. Final setback is the distance from the column’s outer edge to each radial edge of the opening. The model samples the path every 2 mm or less and calculates signed clearance between each component’s plan footprint and the full-height column. Negative values indicate approximate overlap in plan.
  • 90° corner fit. Lower the left and right tabletop panels first. Then fold the last panel to the right in one motion. It stacks beside the right panel with a 4 mm gap. One sector stays open above the arm along the corner bisector. This motion assumes sliding hinges and an extra pivot. Hardware and single-arm stability still need testing.
  • The folding animation uses the original sector and base-arm motions. Internal assembly collision highlighting is disabled in these schematic views because the silhouettes are simplified. Locking, internal clearances, load capacity and stability require separate checks.

Dimensions, travel paths and clearances are model estimates. Specification references include Table 10 and the project targets and Lesson 5.
